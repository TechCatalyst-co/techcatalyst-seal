Independent review attestation

Reviews you can check, not just trust.

TechCatalyst verifies that a business's most recent reviews come from real customers. The seal on their site is checked live against our records and carries an expiry date, like a security certificate.

How verification works

Verified purchase. The business reports a real order; we send the customer a one-time review link.

Audited. Reviews imported from Google, Yelp and others are checked by a TechCatalyst analyst.

Screened. Every review is scored for duplicate devices, bursts and copy-paste text before it counts.

Checking a seal

Click any TechCatalyst seal. You'll land on our verification page, which shows the certificate's status, expiry date, and the website the seal belongs to.

If the address bar doesn't match the domain on that page, the seal was copied.
